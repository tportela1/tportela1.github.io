01 / School & personal project / 2018

Guardian.

Independence for loved ones.
Peace of mind for caregivers.

Why we built it

I worked with Peter Siqueiros and Michael Lewelling to develop Guardian: an Android Wear OS application, Android application, and web application designed to support people living with Alzheimer’s and their caregivers through location updates, reminders, and remote biometric monitoring.

The project began at Hack Arizona in January 2018. After more than 20 hours of coding, our team received Microsoft’s Social Impact Award and Google’s Best Use of Google Cloud Platform award.

What went into it

Java, HTML, CSS, JavaScript, PHP, Google Cloud, Firebase, Firestore, Cloud Functions, and App Engine.

Featured by Google for Education

Google’s customer story documents our team’s work with Google Cloud and Firebase, and the personal motivation behind Guardian.
