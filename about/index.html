A little about me

A career built
on curiosity.

I’m Teresa Portela, a Senior Lead Software Engineer at Capital One.

The path here

I started in help desk support, moved into systems administration, and then into information security. At the University of Arizona, I worked in the College of Public Health and the Information Security Office.

Today, I work in software engineering at Capital One. My earlier work in support, systems, and security is part of the experience I bring with me.

Outside the day job

I’ve always made room for projects beyond my day-to-day work. I founded Lunoid, a small web development studio, and worked with friends on Guardian, a school and personal project supporting people living with Alzheimer’s and their caregivers.

I care about giving back to the community and keeping a record of work that mattered to me—even after that chapter has ended.
